### Added

- QSO tab: log a phone or CW contact by hand. The frequency, band and mode follow the chosen radio; typing a call looks it up (DXCC and what it would be new for, earlier QSOs from the ledger, name, QTH and grid from QRZ.com's XML lookup) and fills in the POTA park when the station is spotted on the dial (a two-fer fills both). Your own POTA, SOTA and grid stay set across QSOs. A logged QSO goes into the logbook sync's ledger as JTDX's do and waits there until Wavelog and QRZ can be reached, so a station away from its LAN logs as usual and sends on its return. The QRZ login is set from the tab (the password is written mode 600 and never shown again). SOTA references are typed in: the SOTA API's terms don't allow this software to fetch spots without the SOTA team's approval.
