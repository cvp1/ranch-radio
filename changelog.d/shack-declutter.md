### Changed

- Mission Control is now **the Shack** (the phone's bottom bar said "Home"; both say Shack now). The route is still `#/home` and `0` still opens it.
- The Shack is quieter. It has no second clock; the header's UTC clock shows local time and the date on hover. Cards are neutral until they have news: no lane colours, no stripe and no pulsing dot on every card, and no tag for a plain state (on, off, idle, quiet, a zero count). Number-key badges show on the card under the pointer, and the keys footer is gone.
- Text on the Shack is larger and brighter, and each card line is one line, ending in an ellipsis rather than wrapping. A card shows at most two lines. The Logbook card no longer prints its address under the counts, and the last QSO shows as `22:24Z` (or its date if it wasn't today). The Grids card names the mode and band only when you've narrowed them.
- A radio, module or packet hub that is off or not connected leaves the Shack's cards. The Offline chip in the needs row already named it.
- The Shack's band strip: a band where every source answered "nothing" shrinks to its name (still a tune button, its facts on hover). The strip's legend folds behind **Key**. A band whose facts are unknown (waiting, failing, stale) stays open, and so does any note saying something is unknown or not set up. Operate and the wall keep the full strip.
- On a phone the Shack opens with what's on the air and what needs you, then Now, the radios, and the band strip below them.
